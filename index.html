Our Story

Yakiniku Bakufu brings the authentic taste of Japanese grilled cuisine to discerning food lovers. Our name "Bakufu" hearkens back to the Edo period, representing our commitment to traditional culinary excellence.

Founded by Master Chef Hiroshi Tanaka, who hails from a long line of yakiniku masters in Osaka, our restaurant specializes in the finest A5 Wagyu beef, sourced directly from renowned regions in Japan.

Every cut of meat is carefully selected for its marbling, texture, and flavor profile, ensuring an unforgettable dining experience that honors Japanese culinary traditions.
